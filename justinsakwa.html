About Justin Sakwa

I see architecture and spatial design not merely as the arrangement of materials, but as a deliberate tool to spark meaningful social and environmental change.

By bridging physical space planning with computational development and digital craft, I seek to design sustainable, functional, and socially conscious environments. Whether thinking through urban interventions, spatial layouts, or interactive digital representations, my objective is always purpose-driven transformation.

Across all platforms, you can find me building and sharing my journey under the handle @Sir_kwa.
